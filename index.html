About

Hi! My name is Junya, and I'm someone who really likes playing around with stuff on computers, or so they say.
The barebones look on this website is intentional.

In the past I have developed small games, written tiny but useful utility programs, and organised hackathons in Europe. Feel free to check some of them out in the Projects section. Oh, and I also really like Java. Really, really like Java. You should look at some of my Minecraft mods!

I like travelling and exploring what the world has to offer, attending hackathons at GitHub HQ in San Francisco and Zhejiang Lakeside University in Hangzhou, and experiencing transit.

I'm currently a 1st year student at Aberystwyth University doing a BSc in Computer Science.
