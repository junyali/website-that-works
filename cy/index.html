Ynghylch

Helo! Junya dw i, ac rwy'n rhywun sy'n hoffi iawn o chwarae o gwmpas gyda phethau ar gyfrifiaduron.
Mae arddull syml y wefan hon yn fwriadol.

Rwyf wedi datblygu gemau bach, creu rhaglenni bach defnyddiol, a threfnu hacathoniadau yn Ewrop. Mae croeso i chi edrych ar rai ohonynt yn yr adran Prosiectau. Hefyd, dw i'n hoffi iawn o Java. Dw i'n hoffi iawn, iawn o Java. Dylech chi edrych ar rai o'm heddasiadau Minecraft i!

Dw i'n hoffi teithio ac archwilio beth sydd gan y byd i'w gynnig. Rwyf hefyd wedi mynychu hacathons yn Pencadlys GitHub yn San Francisco a Prifysgol Zhejiang Lakeside yn Hangzhou.

Rwyf yn fy mlwyddyn gyntaf yn Prifysgol Aberystwyth yn astudio BSc Gwyddorau Cyfrifiadurol.

Fy Prosiectau

  • Flourish

    4ydd Ebrill 2025

    The first game I've ever created - a languageless exploration game featuring different seasons that the player adventures through, with a harvesting and planting system similar to Stardew Valley. Available on Itch, may be released on Steam in the future. Demonstrated at a pop-up cafe in Shanghai 4-11 April.

  • Arsenal and Anvil

    31ain Mai 2025

    Probably the first decent Minecraft mod I've made. A basic 'Quality of Life' modification that adds a variety of new weapon and tool types in Minecraft. Not the most proud of it, the codebase is very messy and hardcoded, but it does the job for what I wanted to achieve at the time.

  • warp-tui

    2il Rhagfyr 2025

    A terminal user interface wrapper for Cloudflare warp-cli built using Python Textual. This was also my first time interacting with Python Packaging and making the program more accessible online. The codebase is also incredibly messy and not workable for future additions. Partly inspired by nmtui, as every day at school I would have to connect to Cloudflare WARP and would get tired of manually typing out warp-cli commands trying to battle the network's web filtering systems.

  • fsa-maps

    23ain Rhagfyr 2025

    A simple web application that visualises UK Food Standards Agency (FSA) food hygiene ratings on an interactive map. This was my first time playing around with FastAPI and trying to optimise things like rendering so many waypoints at a zoom level.

  • Jumper

    31ain Awst 2025

    No, this is not the Geometry Dash level, but there is indeed a lot of jumping involved. A swiftly-developed endless platform game that was kind of inspired by the offline Google dinosaur game. The game has synchronisation issues and therefore your gameplay may vary depending on your hardware specifications and device's resolution, and platforms are randomly generated with no bias therefore some jumps are physically impossible. But it's still fun to play in school!
